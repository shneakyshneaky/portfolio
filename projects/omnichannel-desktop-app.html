Omnichannel Desktop App

CCaaS B2B Software for CX

Role

Design system
UX/UI

Client

Contact Center services startup

Team

Crystal, Emma, Joe, Victor, Ozgun

To meet the demands of modern customer service, we set out to create an omnichannel support platform that unified Voice, Chat Messaging, Voicemail, and Email within a single application. The goal was to deliver a holistic agent view enabling seamless and efficient support across all channels. I collaborated with other designers and product managers to deliver a consolidated desktop app. The designers and I handled separate areas of this project — I led the design of general session navigation, Chat Messaging, and Voicemail.

Objective

Reduce tool fragmentation and app-switching across communication channels

Create a holistic agent workspace that supports multiple, concurrent interactions

Challenge

Agents were frequently required to switch between multiple disconnected applications to access information and respond to customers. This slowed response times, increased cognitive load, and reduced customer satisfaction. The core UX challenge was to unify all channels into a single intuitive interface without overwhelming agents.

Solution

I focused on designing comprehensive user flows while asking questions on how our products interact in a new context. I had multiple review sessions with the product managers to discuss missing use cases to fully flesh out our user journeys. We found many scenarios to cover missing contact points in the app as these are new situations not covered in the standalone products. I introduced a session tab bar that allowed agents to seamlessly navigate between active conversations and provide crucial data. I designed the Chat and Voicemail experience ensuring they functioned cohesively within the broader system and within the app. Additionally, I designed the experience for handling multiple concurrent interactions, such as managing a phone call and chat within a single session, enabling agents to stay focused and responsive without losing context.

Feature Highlight: Real-Time Announcement System

Problem: Internal communication between admins, supervisors, and agents was previously handled via email or third-party tools, leading to missed updates and operational misalignment.

Solution: In collaboration with a Product Manager, I led the design and implementation of a real-time announcement feature, built directly into the agent desktop application.

Agent experience

  • I added in-app notification for critical updates, alerts, or announcements.
  • I emphasized how these alerts should be non-disruptive to the interface while agents are in active sessions.
  • The ability to view and manage announcements were handled in a notification menu

Admin experience

  • I designed a setting addition to the back-end interface for creating, targeting, and scheduling announcements
  • There were options to define the duration, recipient scope, and message priority.
  • I found a missing feature neccessary to complete the entire user journey and support long-term efficiency. We added an archival system for storing expired messages and the ability enable its reuse.

Outcome

This feature improved:

  • Real-time agent awareness during a fast-paced environment and reducing communication gaps
  • Operational alignment across the platforms without relying on third-party systems
  • Communication efficiency for admins through lifecycle management

Results and Impact

Consolidated multiple channels into one streamlined agent experience

Reduce time spent switching between tools and improved agent productivity

Enhanced multi-tasking with concurrent session management

We initially planned on adding email to the centralized desktop app but due to scope and time constraints, we pushed it to phase 2. In the end, the app delivered a high-performance, scalable support experience which led to faster resolution times and better customer service across the board.
